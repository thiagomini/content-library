# Content Library

## Glossary

- **Content Item** — one piece of community material (a lesson, a recording, a post, …) that lives in the community platform and is listed in the Content Library. Each item links back to where it lives.
- **Content Library** — the page that lists every Content Item, newest first.
- **Content Type** — the open-ended category of a Content Item (`lesson`, `blueprint`, `office-hours`, …). There is no fixed set; it is displayed verbatim.
- **Published date** — the day a Content Item was published in the community platform. Items are ordered by it, newest first.
- **Data source** — the frozen local JSON file every Content Item comes from. It is read-only; the app never edits it and never relies on its order.
